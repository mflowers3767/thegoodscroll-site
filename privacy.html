The Good Scroll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing. The Good Scroll has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of our own. Everything the app knows about you lives on your device and nowhere else.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen topics
  • Cards you've seen and saved
  • Your Daily Drop streak
  • Your reminder preference

This data never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No personal information collected
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no data selling — ever
  • No data shared with third parties by us

Third-party content

Some cards play videos through the official YouTube embedded player. When a video plays, YouTube (Google) may process standard data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). We never send YouTube anything about you.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Children

The Good Scroll does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date. Since we collect nothing, changes are unlikely.
